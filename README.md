Tools of the Apocalypse is a Fabric mod that adds a handful of Urban Weaponry that can pair up with your next Zombie/Parasite/Sculk Invasion adventure!

Get a Taste | The Baseball Bat is an item you can craft relativly quickly. With a handful of Flint and a couple pieces of iron, you can upgrade it into a Spiked Baseball Bat, ready to deal even more damage and retain a higher durability.

Where's the Loot? |

Tier 1 Tools - Found in Village Armorer Chests

    Scrap Pickaxe
    Scrap Hatchet
    Scrap Crowbar

Tier 2 Tools - Found in Monster Room Dungeon Chests

    Baton
    Bush Knife
    Machete
    Fireaxe

Tier 3 Tools - Found in Stronghold Chests

    Tactical Pickaxe
    Tactical Hatchet
    Tactical Crowbar
    Tactical Fireaxe
    Sledgehammer
    Katana

Solid Swaps | Tools of the Apocalypse can replace Picks, Axes, & Swords up to and a little past Gold Level. If there's enough demand I can make the tools completely replace Vanilla in purpose, but without the demand, I'm happy to leave it as is.

Better Combat | This mod comes with Better Combat compatibilty built-in and it's strongly recommended that you use it alongside TotA.

Built for Modpack Developers First | TotA really doesn't fit into Vanilla without the careful hand of a thoughtful developer. For this purpose, TotA's MIT source-code is as neat as I can make it for you to parse through at your leisure.

Note | The mod comes with some Vanilla Loot Tables injected already, however it's fully expected that you as a Modpack Developer tweak these tables to add TotA's gear into appropriate locations.
